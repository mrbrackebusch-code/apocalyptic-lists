## Take a hazard out of play

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R11 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Read `hazardRoster`. Remove its first Sprite **and keep the value returned by that block** in `removed`. Submit the shorter roster, then banish `removed`.

Use the returned Sprite for both parts of this job. Removing it from the roster changes the list; ``||apocalypse(noclick):banish removed||`` removes that same actor from play.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `hazardRoster`, `removed`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.
