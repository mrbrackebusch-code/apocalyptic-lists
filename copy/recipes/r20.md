## Reverse the move order

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R20 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Reverse the entire existing `enemyMoves` list and submit it. Keep the values and targets as they were.

Predict the new first and last moves before trying your code.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `enemyMoves`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

Only the order of `enemyMoves` reverses. Enemy values and target references keep their positions.
