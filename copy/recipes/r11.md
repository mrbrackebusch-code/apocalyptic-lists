## Take a hazard out of play

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R11 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Read `hazardRoster`. Remove its first Sprite **and keep the value returned by that block** in `removed`. Submit the shorter roster, then banish `removed`.

Use the returned Sprite for both parts of this job. Removing it from the roster changes the list; ``||apocalypse(noclick):banish removed||`` removes that same actor from play.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `hazardRoster`, `removed`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R11-blocks -->

**Try your code.** Walk the panda to **Banish first** (the Primary altar) and press **A** once. Watch the altar's changes to the pieces and actors. Then walk to the **Release time** crystal at the top and press **A**. Watch the battle and the outcome at the bottom.

The roster loses its first Sprite, and that *same real actor* is banished from the world.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R11-before-after -->

If the result differs, use **B** in open space and choose **Retry this setup**, revise your code, and use the altar again. After a code edit or restart, the room returns to its starting lists; run your current altar code again.

**Need a reminder?** Press **B** beside the altar for its list-operation explanation. For the exact strategy for this setup, press **B** in open space, choose **Altar help + room strategy** with **A**, and use **Left / Right** to turn pages. Beside a moment pad, **A** selects its action and **B** watches it. Use **A** at the hourglass before releasing time to change animation speed.

After success, walk to the bottom **Exit** gate and press **A** to enter the next room, then follow {{recipe:R12}}. This link keeps your current project.
