## Add a final attack

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R04 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Read ``||apocalypse(noclick):Ally move list||`` into `allyMoves`. Use ``||arrays(noclick):add value to end||`` to append `Strike`, then submit it with ``||apocalypse(noclick):use allyMoves as Ally move list||``.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R04-blocks -->

Appending creates one new entry after the last existing entry. The room has already supplied the power and target at that moment; this job changes only the move list.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `allyMoves`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

**Try your code.** Walk the panda to **Append** (the Primary altar) and press **A** once. Watch the altar's changes to the pieces and actors. Then walk to the **Release time** crystal at the top and press **A**. Watch the battle and the outcome at the bottom.

One `Strike` appears at the end of `allyMoves`. The other allied lists keep their original lengths.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R04-before-after -->

If the result differs, use **B** in open space and choose **Retry this setup**, revise your code, and use the altar again. After a code edit or restart, the room returns to its starting lists; run your current altar code again.

**Need a reminder?** Press **B** beside the altar for its list-operation explanation. For the exact strategy for this setup, press **B** in open space, choose **Altar help + room strategy** with **A**, and use **Left / Right** to turn pages. Beside a moment pad, **A** selects its action and **B** watches it. Use **A** at the hourglass before releasing time to change animation speed.

After success, walk to the bottom **Exit** gate and press **A** to enter the next room, then follow {{recipe:R05}}. This link keeps your current project.
