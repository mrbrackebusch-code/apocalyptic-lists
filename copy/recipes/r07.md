## Remove one shield

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R07 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Read `enemyMoves` and the `marked` index. Use ``||arrays(noclick):remove value at||`` to remove the marked move, then submit the shorter enemy move list.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R07-blocks -->

Removing an entry shifts later entries toward earlier moments. This block can also return the removed value; here the room needs only the deletion, so you do not have to keep that value.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `enemyMoves`, `marked`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

The marked position comes from this setup. Selecting a moment with **A** does not change that marker.

**Try your code.** Walk the panda to **Remove Ward** (the Primary altar) and press **A** once. Watch the altar's changes to the pieces and actors. Then walk to the **Release time** crystal at the top and press **A**. Watch the battle and the outcome at the bottom.

The marked enemy `Ward` is gone. The enemy move list is shorter by one, with later items shifted toward earlier moments.
