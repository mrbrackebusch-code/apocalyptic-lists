## Two stations, one changing list

Keep your earlier room code. Add **Primary** and **Secondary** ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R24 station Primary||`` events, and choose the matching station in each dropdown. **Primary** prepends `Wait` to `enemyMoves` and submits it. **Secondary** reads the current list and replaces the move at its marked index with `Wait`, then submits it.

Plan the order before you play. The first job changes every later move's index; the second job's marker names the cell **after** that shift.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `enemyMoves`, `shifted`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R24-blocks -->

**Try your code.** Use **Delay** (Primary), then **Rewrite** (Secondary), before choosing Run. Watch the highlighted cells and the actors, then press **B**, choose **Run the plan**, and press **A**.

After **Primary**, the enemy move list grows by one and existing moves shift right. After **Secondary**, the post-shift marked `Strike` becomes `Wait`. Do these in that order before running combat.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R24-before-after -->

If the result differs, choose **Retry this setup** from the B menu, revise your code, and try the station again. After a code edit or restart the room returns to its starting lists; run your current station code again.

After success, use the **Exit** gate to enter the next room, then follow {{recipe:R25}}. This link keeps your current project.
