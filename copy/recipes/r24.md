## Two altars, one changing list

Keep your earlier room code. Add **Primary** and **Secondary** ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R24 station Primary||`` events, and choose the matching station in each dropdown. **Primary** prepends `Wait` to `enemyMoves` and submits it. **Secondary** reads the current list and replaces the move at its marked index with `Wait`, then submits it.

Use the altars in order. The first job changes every later move's index; the second job's marker names the entry **after** that shift. Selecting a moment does not change this setup marker.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `enemyMoves`, `shifted`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R24-blocks -->

**Try your code.** Walk to **Delay** (Primary) and press **A**, then to **Rewrite** (Secondary) and press **A**. Use the altars in that order. Watch the altar's changes to the pieces and actors. Then walk to the **Release time** crystal at the top and press **A**. Watch the battle and the outcome at the bottom.

After **Primary**, the enemy move list grows by one and existing moves shift toward later moments. After **Secondary**, the post-shift marked `Strike` becomes `Wait`. Do these in that order before running combat.

If the result differs, use **B** in open space and choose **Retry this setup**, revise your code, and use the altar again. After a code edit or restart, the room returns to its starting lists; run your current altar code again.

**Need a reminder?** Press **B** beside the altar for its list-operation explanation. For the exact strategy for this setup, press **B** in open space, choose **Altar help + room strategy** with **A**, and use **Left / Right** to turn pages. Beside a moment pad, **A** selects its action and **B** watches it. Use **A** at the hourglass before releasing time to change animation speed.

After success, walk to the bottom **Exit** gate and press **A** to enter the next room, then follow {{recipe:R25}}. This link keeps your current project.
