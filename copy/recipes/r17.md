## Transfer a reserve move

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R17 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Take the final move out of `sourceMoves` and keep the returned string in `removed`. Append that exact value to `allyMoves`. Submit both the shortened reserve and the extended allied list.

The reserve's final move can change between setups. Let the returned value choose what you transfer.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `sourceMoves`, `allyMoves`, `removed`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R17-blocks -->

**Try your code.** Walk the panda to **Use reserve** (the Primary altar) and press **A** once. Watch the altar's changes to the pieces and actors. Then walk to the **Release time** crystal at the top and press **A**. Watch the battle and the outcome at the bottom.

The source/reserve list loses its last move; that exact string is now the final move of `allyMoves`.
