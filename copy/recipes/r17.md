## Transfer a reserve move

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R17 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Take the final move out of `sourceMoves` and keep the returned string in `transferred`. Append that exact value to `allyMoves`. Submit both the shortened reserve and the extended allied list.

The reserve's final move can change between attempts. Let the returned value choose what you transfer.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `sourceMoves`, `allyMoves`, `removed`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R17-blocks -->

**Try your code.** Walk to **Use reserve** (the Primary station) and press **A** once. Watch the highlighted cells and the actors, then press **B**, choose **Run the plan**, and press **A**.

The source/reserve list loses its last move; that exact string is now the final move of `allyMoves`.

If the result differs, choose **Retry this setup** from the B menu, revise your code, and try the station again. After a code edit or restart the room returns to its starting lists; run your current station code again.
