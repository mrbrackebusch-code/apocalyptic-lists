## Insert a shield

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R05 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Read `allyMoves` and the `marked` index. Use ``||arrays(noclick):insert at||`` to insert `Ward` at the marked position, then submit the allied move list.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R05-blocks -->

Inserting shifts the old entry and every following entry toward later moments. Compare the lengths before and after: this list grows by one.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `allyMoves`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

The marked position comes from this setup. Selecting a moment with **A** does not change that marker.

**Try your code.** Walk the panda to **Insert Ward** (the Primary altar) and press **A** once. Watch the altar's changes to the pieces and actors. Then walk to the **Release time** crystal at the top and press **A**. Watch the battle and the outcome at the bottom.

`Ward` appears at the marked insertion position; all later allied moves shift toward later moments.

If the result differs, use **B** in open space and choose **Retry this setup**, revise your code, and use the altar again. After a code edit or restart, the room returns to its starting lists; run your current altar code again.

**Need a reminder?** Press **B** beside the altar for its list-operation explanation.
