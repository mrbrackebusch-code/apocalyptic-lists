## Insert a shield

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R05 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Read `allyMoves` and the `marked` index. Use ``||arrays(noclick):insert at||`` to insert `Ward` at the marked position, then submit the allied move list.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R05-blocks -->

Inserting shifts the old cell and every following cell one place to the right. Compare the lengths before and after: this list grows by one.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `allyMoves`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

**Try your code.** Walk to **Insert Ward** (the Primary station) and press **A** once. Watch the highlighted cells and the actors, then press **B**, choose **Run the plan**, and press **A**.

`Ward` appears at the marked insertion position; all later allied moves shift one index to the right.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R05-before-after -->

If the result differs, choose **Retry this setup** from the B menu, revise your code, and try the station again. After a code edit or restart the room returns to its starting lists; run your current station code again.

After success, use the **Exit** gate to enter the next room, then follow {{recipe:R06}}. This link keeps your current project.
