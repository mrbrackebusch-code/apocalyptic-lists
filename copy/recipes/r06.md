## Delay the enemy

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R06 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Read `enemyMoves`. Use ``||arrays(noclick):insert at beginning||`` to put `Wait` at its beginning, then submit it.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R06-blocks -->

The new entry becomes index 0. The former first move shifts to index 1, and every later move shifts too. The value and target lists keep their existing positions.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `enemyMoves`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

**Try your code.** Walk the panda to **Delay** (the Primary altar) and press **A** once. Watch the altar's changes to the pieces and actors. Then walk to the **Release time** crystal at the top and press **A**. Watch the battle and the outcome at the bottom.

A new `Wait` becomes the first enemy move. The original first `Strike` becomes the second move.

If the result differs, use **B** in open space and choose **Retry this setup**, revise your code, and use the altar again. After a code edit or restart, the room returns to its starting lists; run your current altar code again.

**Need a reminder?** Press **B** beside the altar for its list-operation explanation. For the exact strategy for this setup, press **B** in open space, choose **Altar help + room strategy** with **A**, and use **Left / Right** to turn pages. Beside a moment pad, **A** selects its action and **B** watches it. Use **A** at the hourglass before releasing time to change animation speed.
