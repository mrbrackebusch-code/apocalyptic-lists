## Delay the enemy

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R06 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Read `enemyMoves`. Use ``||arrays(noclick):insert at beginning||`` to put `Wait` at its beginning, then submit it.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R06-blocks -->

The new cell becomes index 0. The former first move shifts to index 1, and every later move shifts too. The value and target lists keep their existing positions.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `enemyMoves`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

**Try your code.** Walk to **Delay** (the Primary station) and press **A** once. Watch the highlighted cells and the actors, then press **B**, choose **Run the plan**, and press **A**.

A new `Wait` occupies enemy index 0. The original first `Strike` shifts to index 1.
