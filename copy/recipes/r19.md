## Choose one hazard at random

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R19 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Choose an index from **0** through **hazardRoster length − 1**, using ``||math(noclick):pick random||``. Retrieve that Sprite and hold it. Keep both roster entries.

Either actor is a valid random choice. Make one selection for each station press.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `hazardRoster`, `randomIndex`, `selected`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R19-blocks -->

**Try your code.** Walk to **Hold one** (the Primary station) and press **A** once. Watch the highlighted cells and the actors, then press **B**, choose **Run the plan**, and press **A**.

Exactly one roster actor is held. Either random choice is correct, and both Sprite references remain in the roster.
