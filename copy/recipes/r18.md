## Transfer a number

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R18 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Remove the marked number from `enemyValues`, keeping the returned number in `transferred`. Append it to `allyValues`, then submit both lists.

Check both lengths and the transferred value before running the battle.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `enemyValues`, `allyValues`, `marked`, `stolen`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R18-blocks -->

**Try your code.** Walk to **Steal power** (the Primary station) and press **A** once. Watch the highlighted cells and the actors, then press **B**, choose **Run the plan**, and press **A**.

`enemyValues` loses its marked number, and `allyValues` gains that same number at the end.

If the result differs, choose **Retry this setup** from the B menu, revise your code, and try the station again. After a code edit or restart the room returns to its starting lists; run your current station code again.

After success, use the **Exit** gate to enter the next room, then follow {{recipe:R19}}. This link keeps your current project.
