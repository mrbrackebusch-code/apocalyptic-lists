## Hold every hazard

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R13 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Read `hazardRoster` and use ``||loops(noclick):for element||`` to visit each Sprite. Put ``||apocalypse(noclick):hold actor||`` inside that loop, using the loop's `actor` value.

The loop gives you one list member at a time, from first to last. Hold each member without removing its list entry.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `hazardRoster`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.
