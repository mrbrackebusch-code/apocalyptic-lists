## Change one move

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R03 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Now change a value in an existing list. Set `enemyMoves` to ``||apocalypse(noclick):Enemy move list||`` and `marked` to ``||apocalypse(noclick):marked index||``.

<!-- AP_MEDIA id=R03-blocks -->

Use ``||arrays(noclick):set value at||`` to put `Wait` at that index, then ``||apocalypse(noclick):use enemyMoves as Enemy move list||``. The existing entry changes; the list keeps the same number of entries. The room gives you a copy to work on, so the final **use** block submits your edited list.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `enemyMoves`, `marked`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.

The marked position comes from this setup. Selecting a moment with **A** does not change that marker.

**Try your code.** Walk the panda to **Rewrite** (the Primary altar) and press **A** once. Watch the altar's changes to the pieces and actors. Then walk to the **Release time** crystal at the top and press **A**. Watch the battle and the outcome at the bottom.

The marked enemy `Strike` becomes `Wait`. No item is inserted or removed.
