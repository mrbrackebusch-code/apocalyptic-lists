## Match two lists by index

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R25 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Inspect the allied and enemy move lists at each current index. Change the allied cell to `Ward` only when the allied move is `Wait` **and** the opposing enemy move is `Strike`.

Traverse the current allied length, then submit the allied list. Preserve the other `Wait` cells and all existing attacks and healing moves.

Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `allyMoves`, `enemyMoves`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.
