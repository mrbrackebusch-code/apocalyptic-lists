## Match two lists by index

Keep your earlier room code. Add ``||apocalypse(noclick):on room R25 station Primary||`` from ``||apocalypse(noclick):Apocalyptic Lists||``. Put this room's work inside that event. Use ``||variables(noclick):Make a Variable||`` for any new names in this construction: `allyMoves`, `enemyMoves`. Reuse a name that already exists in your project.
